# Privacy Policy - personal Google Calendar integration

This application is a private, single-user integration used by its owner to read and
manage their own Google Calendar through the Google Calendar API.

- **Data accessed**: only the Google Calendar data of the Google account that explicitly
  authorized the application.
- **Storage**: OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's machine. Calendar data
  is not copied to, or persisted on, any server operated by the application author.
- **Sharing**: no data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties. No advertising or
  analytics services are used.
- **Revocation**: access can be revoked at any time at
  https://myaccount.google.com/permissions, which immediately ends the application's access.
- **Limited Use**: the application's use of Google user data is limited to providing the
  calendar functionality described above.
